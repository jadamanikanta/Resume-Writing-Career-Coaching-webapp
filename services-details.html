FAQ

Questions You May Have.

Quick answers about how our career services work.

How do I know which service is right for me?

If you're applying now, start with a resume and cover letter. If you're unsure of your direction, begin with career coaching. You can also contact us and we'll recommend the best fit for your goals.

Will my resume be ATS-friendly?

Yes. Every resume is written with clean formatting and relevant keywords so it reads well for both applicant tracking systems and human recruiters.

Can I combine multiple services?

Absolutely. Many clients pair a resume with LinkedIn optimization and interview coaching. Our packages bundle these services at a better value.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your current resume (if you have one), the roles you're targeting, and a few details about your achievements. We guide you through the rest.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. Your career details are used only to deliver your service and are never shared without your permission.

What if I want changes after delivery?

Each written service includes revision rounds, so we can refine the work until it represents you well.
