Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CAREERLYN collects, uses, protects, and manages information when you use our website and services.

Last Updated: October 2026
01

Introduction

CAREERLYN respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide while using our website and career services.

This policy explains what information may be collected, why it is collected, how it may be used, and the choices available to you.

02

Information We Collect

Depending on the services you use, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Name and contact information.
  • Email address and telephone number.
  • Professional and career information.
  • Resume, CV, LinkedIn, and employment information.
  • Information submitted through forms or coaching requests.
  • Payment and transaction-related information where applicable.
03

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically by the website or its supporting services.

  • Browser and device information.
  • IP address or approximate location information.
  • Pages visited and general website usage information.
  • Referring pages and interaction information.

This information may be used to improve website performance, security, usability, and overall user experience.

04

How We Use Your Information

Information provided to CAREERLYN may be used for purposes including:

  • Providing and managing requested services.
  • Creating and improving career documents and coaching deliverables.
  • Communicating about appointments, projects, and service updates.
  • Processing payments and maintaining transaction records.
  • Responding to questions and support requests.
  • Improving our website, services, and customer experience.
  • Maintaining website security and preventing misuse.
05

Resume and Career Information

Information contained in resumes, CVs, professional profiles, questionnaires, and coaching materials may be used solely as necessary to provide the career services requested by you.

Clients should avoid providing sensitive personal information that is not necessary for the requested service.

06

Information Sharing

CAREERLYN does not sell your personal information for monetary gain.

Information may be shared with trusted service providers when necessary to operate the website, process payments, provide communication services, or deliver requested services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of CAREERLYN and its users.

07

Cookies and Similar Technologies

CAREERLYN or third-party services used on the website may use cookies or similar technologies to remember preferences, understand website usage, maintain functionality, and improve the user experience.

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

08

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

09

Data Retention

Personal information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, meet contractual obligations, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

10

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the ability to request access, correction, or deletion of certain information.

You may also contact us regarding questions about how your information is handled.

11

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, platforms, or services. CAREERLYN is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external services.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of third-party websites before providing them with personal information.

12

Children's Privacy

CAREERLYN services are intended for individuals who can lawfully enter into agreements for the services offered. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children through our website.

13

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in our services, technology, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

Any updated version will be published on this page together with a revised "Last Updated" date.

14

Contact Us
